Rapidity screen reader testing

Rapidity is a spaceship operations simulator built accessibility-first: the whole game is instruments, and it must be fully playable with a screen reader and no screen. Before the real panel gets built, two interface patterns need a JAWS verdict.

Two tests, six questions each, about 30 minutes. Your everyday JAWS and Chrome setup, unmodified.

  1. Test 1 — a live gauge: three announcement policies to compare.
  2. Test 2 — a panel region: single-letter commands scoped to a region, Escape to leave.
  3. Report form: autosaves as you type; one button turns it into plain text to send to Jeremy.

Blunt is useful. "Works, but I'd hate it after an hour" is exactly the kind of answer this needs.

Begin with Test 1. Thanks.

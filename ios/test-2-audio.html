Test 2 — sound and the mute switch

The game speaks through sound as much as speech — engine pitch, alert tones. The worry: iPhones can silence web audio with the mute switch or Action button, which for a blind player would mean losing half the game without knowing why. The two buttons below make the same steady tone through two different plumbing routes; the whole test is finding out what your mute control does to each.

audio: not started

Questions (answer on the report form)

  1. 2.1 — Phone unmuted, VoiceOver running as usual: start route A. Do you hear the tone alongside VoiceOver?
  2. 2.2 — While A plays, mute the phone (switch or Action button). Does the tone keep playing or stop?
  3. 2.3 — Reload the page with the phone already muted, then start route A. Any sound?
  4. 2.4 — Repeat 2.1 to 2.3 with route B. Any differences from A?
  5. 2.5 — With a tone playing: lock the phone, then unlock. Then switch to another app and back. What survives, and what does the status line above say afterward?
  6. 2.6 — Does the tone fight VoiceOver at all — ducking, garbled speech, volume battles?
